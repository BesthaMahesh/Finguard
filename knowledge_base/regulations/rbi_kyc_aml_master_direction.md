# Regulatory Direction: KYC & AML Standards (RBI & International Baselines)
Document ID: REG-KYC-AML-2024
Version: 4.0
Source: Reserve Bank of India & Basel Committee Regulatory Guidelines

## 1. High-Risk Customer Categorization
Entities and individuals conducting wire transfers and cash-out operations exceeding specified thresholds must undergo Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD).
- Customer accounts exhibiting transactions inconsistent with declared financial profile.
- Non-face-to-face account operations without biometric or multi-factor authentication.

## 2. Red Flag Indicators for Banking Intermediaries
- Customer is reluctant to provide complete identity documentation.
- Multiple high-value transfers conducted within identical time steps (structuring).
- Rapid transfer of funds across multiple intermediary accounts without clear economic rationale.

## 3. Preservation of Records
- Audit records, transaction traces, and investigation findings must be maintained for a statutory period of not less than 10 years.
- All machine learning risk decisions must be explainable upon request by statutory bank examiners.
